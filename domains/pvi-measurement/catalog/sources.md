# Research references

References are selected anchors, **not** a claim that each catalog metric has been individually validated in the cited source.

The machine-readable [evidence library](measurement-evidence.json) adds study-specific citations, appraisal notes, factor/item evidence, and exact standards mappings. The absence of a study from that curated file means **not yet appraised**, not “no study exists.”

- **[iso9241]** ISO (2018). [ISO 9241-11:2018: Usability—Definitions and concepts](https://www.iso.org/standard/63500.html). *Use:* Usability framework, not validation of a particular metric.
- **[faa2016]** Yeh et al. (2016). [Human Factors Considerations in the Design and Evaluation of Flight Deck Displays and Controls, V2; Appendix C](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/12411). *Use:* FAA evaluation procedures; objective and subjective metrics; not binding certification criteria.
- **[faaAC]** FAA (2013). [AC 25.1302-1: Installed Systems and Equipment for Use by the Flightcrew](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/1021086). *Use:* Advisory circular for specified transport-aircraft contexts.
- **[lewis1995]** Lewis (1995). [IBM Computer Usability Satisfaction Questionnaires: Psychometric Evaluation and Instructions for Use](https://doi.org/10.1080/10447319509526110). *Use:* ASQ and PSSUQ psychometrics.
- **[sus2008]** Bangor, Kortum & Miller (2008). [An Empirical Evaluation of the System Usability Scale](https://doi.org/10.1080/10447310802205776). *Use:* SUS cross-product evaluation.
- **[umux2013]** Lewis, Utesch & Maher (2013). [UMUX-LITE: When There's No Time for the SUS](https://doi.org/10.1145/2470654.2481287). *Use:* Two-item usability psychometrics.
- **[nasatlx]** Hart & Staveland (1988). [Development of NASA-TLX (Task Load Index): Results of Empirical and Theoretical Research](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-4115(08)62386-9). *Use:* Six-dimension perceived workload.
- **[nasaTLXsite]** NASA Ames (n.d.). [NASA Task Load Index (TLX): Official Instrument and Instructions](https://www.nasa.gov/human-systems-integration-division/nasa-task-load-index-tlx/). *Use:* Administration and source materials.
- **[sagat]** Endsley (1995). [Measurement of Situation Awareness in Dynamic Systems](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872095779049499). *Use:* Direct scenario-query methodology, intrusion and validity.
- **[satheory]** Endsley (1995). [Toward a Theory of Situation Awareness in Dynamic Systems](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872095779049543). *Use:* SA construct framework.
- **[pilotstick]** van Weelden et al. (2023). [Differentiating Workload Using Pilot's Stick Input in a Virtual Reality Flight Task](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09619). *Use:* Pilot inceptor activity with 2-back manipulation; small-sample preliminary.
- **[niewind]** Niewind (2012). [Pilot Gain and the Workload Buildup Flight Test Technique: A Closer Investigation of Pilot Inceptor Workload](https://elib.dlr.de/88208/). *Use:* Aggressiveness, duty cycle and PIW operationalization.
- **[nasaF18]** Hanson et al. (2014). [Complexity and Pilot Workload Metrics for the Evaluation of Adaptive Flight Controls on a Full Scale Piloted Aircraft](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20140005730). *Use:* Flight control activity and aggressive input in F-18 research aircraft.
- **[hebbar2021]** Hebbar et al. (2021). [Correlation Between Physiological and Performance-Based Metrics to Estimate Pilots' Cognitive Workload](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.555446). *Use:* Stick control measures and concurrent physiological data.
- **[gaze2025]** Ayala et al. (2025). [Aviation eye-tracking methodology in a flight simulator](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40257960/). *Use:* Cockpit AOI mapping and recording guidance.
- **[coopHarper]** Cooper & Harper (1969). [The Use of Pilot Rating in the Evaluation of Aircraft Handling Qualities](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19690013177). *Use:* Handling qualities rating—not global usability.
- **[maxdiff]** Louviere, Flynn & Marley (2015). [Best–Worst Scaling: Theory, Methods and Applications](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/bestworst-scaling/). *Use:* Best–worst choice study design, not guaranteed small-n inference.
- **[faaMFD]** Mejdal, McCauley & Beringer (2001). [Human Factors Design Guidelines for Multifunction Displays](https://www.faa.gov/data_research/research/med_humanfacs/oamtechreports/2000s/2001/200117). *Use:* Display interpretation, menu structure and navigation.
- **[simstudy]** Friedrich et al. (2021). [Manual Flight Performance, Gaze and Task Load in Simulator Conditions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10111-020-00663-8). *Use:* Flight telemetry/gaze/workload integration.
- **[isoSusc]** ISO (n.d.). [ISO general measurement framework for visual presentation](https://www.iso.org/committee/53372.html). *Use:* Bibliographic orientation only; verify applicable display standards individually.

## Aviation standards and display/ergonomic methods

- **[mil1472h]** U.S. DoD. [MIL-STD-1472H, Human Engineering](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsdocdetails.aspx?ident_number=36903), 15 Sep 2020; Notice 1, 10 Apr 2026. *Use:* Applicable human-engineering criteria only when the exact revision/clause is invoked and tailored.
- **[mil516d]** U.S. DoD. [MIL-HDBK-516D, Airworthiness Certification Criteria](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsDocDetails.aspx?ident_number=212162), 29 May 2026. *Use:* Certification-basis and means-of-compliance guidance; the handbook cannot itself be cited as a requirement. It supersedes 516C.
- **[mil46855a]** U.S. DoD. [MIL-STD-46855A](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsdocdetails.aspx?ident_number=113938), 24 May 2011; Notice 3, 23 Sep 2025. *Use:* Invoked/tailored human-engineering program, analysis, and test requirements.
- **[mil87213b]** U.S. DoD. [MIL-HDBK-87213B, Electronically/Optically Generated Airborne Displays](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsDocDetails.aspx?ident_number=123715), 2 Mar 2020; Notice 1, 10 Dec 2024. *Use:* Detailed display measurement guidance; sample values/blanks are not automatic requirements.
- **[mil3009]** U.S. DoD. [MIL-STD-3009](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsDocDetails.aspx?ident_number=209352), 2 Feb 2001; Notice 2, 4 Apr 2024. *Use:* NVIS-compatible lighting/display scope.
- **[faaHFv3]** FAA (2024). [Human Factors Considerations in the Design and Evaluation of Flight Deck Displays and Controls, Version 3.0](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/87936). *Use:* Current FAA evaluation reference, not an automatic means of compliance.
- **[faaHFSTD]** FAA (2016). [DOT/FAA/HF-STD-001B](https://hf.tc.faa.gov/publications/2016-12-human-factors-design-standard/full_text.pdf). *Use:* FAA design criteria, not automatically a military-airworthiness requirement.
- **[nasa3001v2f]** NASA (2026). [NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2, Revision F](https://standards.nasa.gov/node/237). *Use:* Supplemental human-spaceflight benchmark unless explicitly invoked.
- **[nasaHWD2017]** Arthur et al. (2017). [Evaluation of a Head-Worn Display System as an Equivalent Head-Up Display for Low Visibility Commercial Operations](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20170007195). *Use:* Multi-method aviation/HWD evidence pattern, not universal thresholds.
- **[borgCR10]** Borg (1998). [Borg’s Perceived Exertion and Pain Scales](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1998-07179-000). *Use:* Perceived-exertion framework; reproduce only under applicable terms.
- **[rula1993]** McAtamney & Corlett (1993). [RULA](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-6870(93)90080-S). *Use:* Upper-limb ergonomic screening, not diagnosis.
- **[reba2000]** Hignett & McAtamney (2000). [REBA](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0003-6870(99)00039-3). *Use:* Whole-body ergonomic screening, not diagnosis.
