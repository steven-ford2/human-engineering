# Source canon, provenance and confidence

## Reading ladder
**Core P0, must engage:** MIL-STD-46855A (official release, applicability/revision verify); FAA HF-STD-004A (2016); Guyton/Kuhlken/Lowe (2025) mission task analysis; Stanton (2006) HTA; Endsley (1993) fighter SA requirements; Militello/Hutton (1998) ACTA; Crandall/Klein/Hoffman (2006) Working Minds; Endsley/Jones (2011) Designing for Situation Awareness; Vicente (1999) Cognitive Work Analysis; Naikar (2013) Work Domain Analysis; Barshi et al. (2016) flight-deck procedures; ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 (2018) for requirements; Leveson (2012) for systems safety.
**Methods supplements:** Kirwan/Ainsworth (1992), Diaper/Stanton (2004), Klein critical-decision sources; Sarter/Woods (1995), Parasuraman/Sheridan/Wickens (2000), Wickens workload; Endsley SAGAT, NASA-TLX and experimental measurement texts.
**Standards and DIDs:** DoD HSI guide, MIL-STD-1472H, MIL-STD-882E, relevant 80745/80746/80747/81399 DIDs, FAA advisory circulars where applicable.

## Direct verified public research entry points
- FAA HF-STD-004A (2016) official publication and downloadable document: https://hf.tc.faa.gov/publications/2016-09-human-factors-engineering-requirements/ . **Source inspected:** public PDF page 11, §§5.1.10.1–5.1.10.2, including task time/simultaneity, criticality, feedback/error, uncertainty/decisions, information required/available and actions. As a FAA document, its 'must' language is **not automatically binding on a DoD contract**.
- Militello & Hutton (1998) publisher record: https://doi.org/10.1080/001401398186108 ; indexed abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9819578/ ; public manuscript: https://web.mit.edu/16.459/www/Militello98.pdf . Core three ACTA interview methods; this source supports practitioner-oriented knowledge elicitation.
- Endsley (1993) fighter SA study: https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327108ijap0302_5 . **Publisher abstract inspected**, not publisher full-text; abstract identifies interviews, GDTA and structured pilot questionnaires to derive/prioritize SA requirements.
- Stanton (2006) HTA review: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apergo.2005.06.003 ; university archive: https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/handle/2438/1423 . **Abstract and archive metadata verified**; applications include interface design, function allocation, workload and error prediction.
- Barshi et al. (2016), NASA/TM-2016-219421: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20160013263 ; full text https://hsi.arc.nasa.gov/publications/Barshi_Procedure_Checklist_Design_NASA_TM_2016.pdf . **Overview and seven-step structure inspected:** triggers, process, issues, solutions, writing, implementation, evaluation/monitoring.
- NASA (2025) further procedure-design conceptual model: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20250004433 . **Abstract inspected**; does not supplant the 2016 seven-step framework.
- Naikar (2013) publisher book outline: https://www.routledge.com/Work-Domain-Analysis-Concepts-Guidelines-and-Cases-1st-Edition/Naikar/p/book/9780805861297 . **Publisher contents inspected**; structural means-ends, abstraction, decomposition and validation.
- Guyton/Kuhlken/Lowe (2025): https://doi.org/10.54941/ahfe1006105 ; [processed full text already in repo](https://github.com/steven-ford2/FighterJets/blob/main/references/task-analyses-mbse.md). **Text examined** for mission/function/task, mission element matrix, task fields, SysML.

## How to distinguish source claims
**[STANDARD]** exact clause with official source/version, jurisdiction and applicability.
**[PRIMARY]** method or empirical claim attributed to research and accessible text scope.
**[ABSTRACT]** abstract or publisher summary, no inference of methods not shown.
**[TEXT]** reviewed selected passage from publicly linked/project source; specify sections.
**[SYNTHESIS]** original engineering integration designed for this course.
**[HYPOTHESIS]** untested teaching-case claim or performance threshold.

## Never do these
- Present draft course framework as a normative requirement or established validated method.
- Treat 1993 fighter research as evidence for every modern fighter system and mission type.
- Claim ACTA by itself proves a cockpit design is safe.
- Use cockpit operational details beyond public/synthetic examples.
- Copy full copyrighted textbook chapters into the GitHub repository without rights.
- Replace original authors' distinctions with an invented unified theory while implying those authors endorsed it.
- Claim that all 87 references have been individually critically appraised; they have NOT.
