# Entry diagnostic / retrieval examination

Use **without notes** before starting, then repeat after Week 8 and Week 16. Approx. 35–50 minutes. The answer key is in a separate file, and questions are not designed as a psychometrically validated test.

## Concepts (1–10)
1. Distinguish a mission objective, function and task with one example each.
2. Identify the error in 'operator needs a 20-mm green indicator'.
3. Explain task, decision, information need and control need using one mode-transition example.
4. Why does the task's *observed action* not establish operator *intent*?
5. What is the distinction between data received, data interpreted and data accepted as authoritative?
6. Contrast nominal procedure and observed work.
7. Explain why a choice of current interface layout could bias an interview.
8. Distinguish an elicited SME statement from a validated requirement.
9. State two failures hidden by a nominal-scenario-only task analysis.
10. Explain the difference between assigned operator authority and the UI availability of a control.

## Methods (11–18)
11. Describe HTA's hierarchy and why plans are essential.
12. When would a GDTA outperform a parameter inventory?
13. Name and distinguish ACTA's three interview techniques.
14. Why is retrospective CDM evidence not direct observation?
15. What does WDA reveal that HTA alone might miss?
16. Name at least two parts of CWA beyond WDA.
17. Under which conditions would you decline to apply a full CWA?
18. Explain the value and limitation of a mission element matrix.

## Design and evaluation (19–30)
19. Construct a command-state sequence from request to achieved outcome.
20. Give an example of a correct displayed value that nevertheless misleads a decision.
21. Distinguish pilot information need from signal provenance.
22. Draft a requirement addressing requested-versus-active ambiguity.
23. How does a procedure design test differ from a checklist-clicks test?
24. Identify two variables that operationalize safe recovery after an inhibited command.
25. Why does fixation count not prove situation awareness?
26. Describe a plausible confound of pupil diameter in an interface test.
27. Why does 'p > .05' not establish equivalence?
28. Describe a case where low workload and worse task performance coexist.
29. Contrast analyst agreement, operational validity and design utility.
30. Identify one red-team challenge that could invalidate your favorite interface concept.

## Applied mini-case (31)
A UI shows mode B as highlighted immediately when the pilot selects it; a system trace reports B is accepted but not yet controlling. In <=300 words, propose (a) a decision-driven information need, (b) control feedback path, (c) one solution-independent requirement, (d) two plausible designs, (e) two primary operational measures, and (f) one misleading candidate measure to reject.

## Scoring guidance
Award partial credit for correctness plus source/assumption awareness. Suggested: items 1–30 at 2 points each; mini-case up to 40; total 100. Diagnostic placement only; numerical bands are locally proposed, not scientifically calibrated.
