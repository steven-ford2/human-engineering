# Instructor, coach and independent-review guide

## Teaching philosophy
Teach **critical method judgment** rather than memorization of acronyms. Every student must explain when a method is appropriate, where it fails, how findings affect system design, and how a subsequent study would demonstrate or disprove the predicted value. The same realistic synthetic case should recur across topics so method outputs can be compared.

## Standard weekly session (self-paced or cohort)
1. **Retrieval (10 min):** ask last week's key distinction with no notes.
2. **Conceptual lecture (45–60 min):** read/perform lesson; separate source-based claims from course hypotheses.
3. **Primary paper inspection (30–60 min):** extract method input/output and one limitation from actual source; annotate page where possible.
4. **Worked analysis (30 min):** reproduce key table/model independent of the provided interpretation.
5. **Scenario lab (60–120 min):** create original artifact; do not substitute reflective prose for a diagram/table.
6. **Red-team (30 min):** ask a peer or AI to attempt disconfirmation; ensure confidential material is excluded.
7. **Review (15–30 min):** self-grade against rubric, record next evidence gap, enter portfolio.

## Socratic probe bank
- Where is the operational decision in this apparently complete task model?
- What would remain if the UI were removed?
- Why would a veteran know the state from a cue a novice ignores?
- Is source validity a function of the sensor, the software model, the communication network or the operator's interpretation?
- When does received/acknowledged/accepted/active/achieved matter?
- How would you discover that the proposed task sequence is wrong?
- What is the strongest alternative explanation?
- Could the proposed measure improve while task success worsens?
- What would a system engineer need to verify before this is implementable?
- Does the source justify the claim, or have we promoted a summary to authority?

## Consistency audit at Weeks 4/8/12/16
At Week 4 require at least one proper plan and event/decision exception. At Week 8 require cognition/constraint insights missed by action lists. At Week 12 require state/authority/source completeness and independently plausible design alternatives. At Week 16 require grounded test constructs and blinded independent reviewer evidence.

## Assessment protocol
Before reading the student's preferred design concept, review their scenario and needs. For each critical requirement choose a random chain and ask its source, context, alternatives and test. For each metric ask which claim it operationalizes and the confounds. For all conflicts require a written disposition and a source basis.

## Using audio commuting time
Lecture may be listened to while commuting; diagrams, data records, answers and judgments must be done later while stationary. A suggested oral recap: 'The single most important decision; the method I used; why the source matters; the evidence I lack; the test that could prove me wrong.' Do not perform interactive training exercises while driving.

## Using AI/Codex responsibly
Good uses: generating blind scenario variants, semantic checks against controlled taxonomy, diagrams from already anonymized/synthetic records, comparison of two independent analyses and retrieval quizzes. Bad uses: inventing operator observations, declaring a method 'validated' after synthetic tests, bypassing required SME review, or uploading employer-controlled artifacts to personal clouds. AI-derived suggestions are **analyst hypotheses** until reviewed.

## Course QA and release checklist
- Every week has goals, substantive method content, worked example, exercise, retrieval, limitation and reading.
- Every case contains an explicit synthetic label and disallowance of platform-specific operating instructions.
- Every source-based statement attributes its source and evidentiary scope (abstract vs full text).
- Every method artifact includes objective and a review/failure criterion.
- Critical source-to-need-to-metric relationships can be reconstructed without author participation.
- All relative links render and files exist.
- A peer who has not written the material can complete a Week 5 GDTA and Week 13 metric lab without clarifying the intended scope.
- Record peer review / field pilot evidence before advertising validated organizational effectiveness.
