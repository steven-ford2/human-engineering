# Assessment system and instructor calibration

## Formative assessment design
Every week yields **an actual inspectable artifact**, a retrieval response produced without references, an adversarial critique, and a short design/test transfer exercise. The curriculum is competency-led, not reading-list-led. Grade method selection and evidence handling as closely as final answers.

## Four-level criterion
0 missing; 1 present but unsupported; 2 defensible with gaps; 3 clear, correct, sourced and independently reproducible. Score each of the 12 existing dimensions in [quality rubric](../practice/quality-rubric.md), with written evidence.

## Six gate tests
1. *Classification:* examples correctly separate task, decision, info need, control need, behavior requirement and empirical measure.
2. *Coverage:* scenarios encompass nominal, exceptional, degraded, concurrent and recovery paths appropriate to importance, with omissions acknowledged.
3. *Elicitation:* cognitive demands grounded in non-leading study records; expertise and alternative interpretations explicit.
4. *Design:* major choice demonstrates a chain from operator intent/decision, through source capability and risk, to a specific testable proposition.
5. *Evaluation:* every critical claimed benefit has a suitable primary outcome, independent ground truth, context and confounds.
6. *Reproducibility:* an independent analyst can interpret the analysis, flag uncertainty and explain its rationale.

## Critique prompts
- What scenario would produce the opposite outcome?
- What information is not available, even if a display represents it?
- How do you know action was achieved instead of only requested?
- Which evidence is observation, elicitation, engineering assumption or inference?
- Which candidate metric could improve even as flightcrew effectiveness declines?
- What extra field or cue is an ergonomic convenience versus a genuine mission constraint?
- What is the smallest additional study needed to resolve the critical uncertainty?

## Midterm (end of Week 8; 90 min)
Provide a new synthetic mode-management scenario. Learner has 60 min to deliver: two-level task hierarchy, three decision records, six information cues with validity/availability distinctions, ACTA prompts, an abstraction hierarchy and method selection rationale. Use remaining 30 min to identify two false inferences, one missing exception, and design two SME verification questions. Assess with criterion rubric, not aesthetic presentation.

## Final capstone and defense
See [capstone spec](./capstone/CAPSTONE.md). A red-team reviewer selects three requirements and traces them to evidence, then three metrics to operational claims. All critical gaps must be documented, not quietly patched.

## Method reliability pilot (for later organizational adoption)
Use the same frozen dossier and definitions for >=2 analysts. Report task and decision **presence/absence**, category labels, relationship matching, missed critical scenarios, and case-specific utility. Blind coding prevents group consensus from masquerading as independent agreement. Measure reliability appropriately for prevalence and sampling; do not assert a universal 'good kappa' threshold. Adjudicate substantive disagreement and update the manual.

## Answer keys
Detailed model-response and diagnostic artifacts are in `assessments/`, separate from the weekly lectures. These are **illustrative** and not guaranteed uniquely correct. A learner may have multiple evidence-backed valid decompositions.
