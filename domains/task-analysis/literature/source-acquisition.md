5. Institutional-library access for peer-reviewed full texts.

## Acquire first

**Tier B (analysis):** Kirwan & Ainsworth, Stanton, Working Minds, ACTA, Endsley 1993/2011, Vicente, Naikar.
**Tier C (downstream):** ISO 29148, INCOSE handbook, FAA flight-deck v2, procedure design, automation/safety papers.

## Legal and information handling
Store DOI URLs, bibliographic entries and original synthesis. Link rather than mirror publisher PDFs absent redistribution permission. Even though FighterJets currently is private, a personal GitHub repository is not an authorized destination for employer material. Publicly released military documents still require source/revision verification.
