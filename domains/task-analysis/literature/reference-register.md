## Review extraction fields
For each P0 item record: (1) bibliographic/revision details, (2) normative status, (3) operator/population, (4) unit of analysis, (5) inputs, (6) elicitation steps and probes, (7) representations and outputs, (8) operational assumptions/constraints, (9) demonstrated evidence and methods validity, (10) reliability/reproducibility, (11) advantages/costs/failure modes, (12) relation to requirements/design/testing, (13) adoption decision.

## Substantive cautions
- Standards establish criteria only to the extent they are applicable and incorporated/tailored. A guide is not automatically a mandatory requirement.
- GDTA information requirements, HTA task steps, CTA expert cues, CWA constraints, STPA safety constraints and interface requirements are different artifacts. Do not flatten them into one column.
- Neither NASA-TLX nor eye-tracking metrics directly prove SA or safe workload allocation.
- Source claims summarized in the register are an orientation; review the actual publication to verify method detail.
